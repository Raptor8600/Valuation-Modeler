# Methodology

This document separates the three kinds of content in the model. Every number in the app carries one of these labels.

| Kind | What it is | Where you check it |
|---|---|---|
| **Sourced fact** | A figure reported by the company (SEC XBRL) or a market feed (Yahoo Finance) | The source tag: XBRL tag, period, fetch time, link to the filing |
| **Formula** | Arithmetic on facts and inputs | `formulas.yaml` / [formulas.md](formulas.md), the Logic page, the "show your work" panels |
| **Judgment call** | A default someone chose (tax rate, ERP, terminal growth, fade shape, …) | [assumptions.md](assumptions.md) and the Assumptions page, each with its live sensitivity |

## 1. Data (sourced facts)

* **Financial statements**: SEC EDGAR XBRL `companyfacts` API, parsed by `valuation/sec_data.py` (the same parser the Excel builder uses) so the app and
  the workbook read identical numbers. Trailing-twelve-month (TTM) figures = latest fiscal year + current year-to-date −
  prior-year year-to-date; each part is listed with its filing.
* **Filings directory**: SEC `submissions` API (latest 10-K, 10-Q and the Item 2.02 earnings 8-K with Exhibit 99.1).
* **Market data**: Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` (price, 5-year beta, ^TNX 10-year yield, analyst revenue-growth consensus).
  Third-party and unofficial; there is no filing to check these against.
* Every live search is kept as a snapshot - the raw provider responses exactly as returned, with request URLs and fetch
  time - in the visitor's session (My searches); it can be downloaded and re-opened later, and replays identically.

## 2. From facts to inputs (formulas)

Non-GAAP bridge (SBC, acquired-intangible amortization and other adjustments, each switchable), D&A used, capex used
(PP&E + capitalized software), the working-capital residual (OCF − NI − D&A − SBC), cash / debt / other claims counted
(each balance-sheet line switchable), diluted share change. See `data.*` entries in [formulas.md](formulas.md).

## 3. Discount rate

CAPM: Ke = Rf + β × ERP; Kd = interest ÷ debt if above Rf else Rf + 1.5%; WACC = We·Ke + Wd·Kd(1 − t) with market-value
equity and book debt. Standard mode uses a direct WACC, defaulting to the CAPM figure rounded to 0.1%. One flat rate for
all years.

## 4. Forecast

Ten explicit years. Automatic fade: growth falls linearly from Year-1 growth (consensus, else TTM) to terminal growth by
Year 10; margin moves linearly from TTM to a target by a target year. Manual Override replaces any year; Scenario selects
editable Bear / Base / Bull rows (defaults ±5pp growth fading to zero, ±3pp margin phased in).

## 5. DCF

UFCF_t = EBIT_t − tax + D&A − capex + ΔNWC, tax only on positive EBIT by default. Discounting end-of-year (or mid-year).
Terminal value: Gordon growth on an explicitly built Year 11 (Year-10 margin, Year-11 working-capital charge), or an exit
multiple on Year-10 EBITDA / UFCF / revenue. EV = Σ PV + PV(TV); equity = EV + net cash; fair value = equity ÷ diluted
shares. A 7×7 sensitivity on WACC × terminal parameter, and a value-over-time path (price at the end of each year).

## 6. Reverse DCF

Solves the constant 10-year revenue growth g that makes model EV equal market EV (price × shares − net cash), with the same
margins, tax, D&A, capex, working capital and terminal method as the DCF. Root finding: a 1% grid from −50% to +150%,
then 40 bisection steps in the first bracket where the sign changes. No sign change → "No solution in range"; more than
one → the lowest root is shown and flagged. A proof schedule rebuilds the years at g and must tie out to market EV.

## 7. IRR

Eleven multiples × trailing / forward. Metric paths come from the DCF lines; the entry multiple is computed from today's
price; the exit multiple defaults to the entry multiple; exit price = multiple × metric per share (+ net cash per share
for EV multiples). IRR on per-share cash flows (−price, dividends, exit price), like Excel's IRR().

## 8. How correctness is established

1. **Excel parity** - the owner's private builder writes the workbook, LibreOffice recalculates it, and every compared value
   across all scenarios (DDOG GAAP / Non-GAAP, synthetic profitable and loss-making companies, every toggle, all 22 IRR
   multiples) is checked against the engine at 0.1% tolerance (`tests/test_excel_parity.py`; counts in
   VERIFICATION_REPORT.md).
2. **Independent reference implementation** - `reference_impl/simple_valuation.py` (plain loops, no shared code, a
   different reverse-DCF formulation and a different IRR algorithm) agrees with the engine on hundreds of random cases.
3. **Live integrity checks** in the app with their numbers (tie-outs, residuals, sensitivity centre, weights, units).
4. **Edge-case tests** - terminal growth ≥ WACC, no-solution reverse DCF, units, zero dividends, negative / zero margins,
   missing data.
5. `VERIFICATION_REPORT.md` - generated by one command, regenerated by CI on every push.
